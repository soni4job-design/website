Your safety, by design

You choose who sees your plan

Same gender is the default when you post — only people like you see it. Switch to Everyone, Men only, or Women only. You're always in control of who can reach you.

You control who joins

Every join request comes to you first. Accept or reject — no one joins without your say. Chat only unlocks after you accept.

Block or report anyone

Block or report any user at any time, no questions asked. All reports are reviewed by our team. A quiet reputation system ensures bad actors fade out on their own.
