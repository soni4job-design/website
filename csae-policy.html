Child Safety Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Meopel is committed to the safety of all users and has a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) in any form.

1. Age Requirement

Meopel is strictly for users aged 18 and above. All accounts are verified via a valid Indian mobile number. Users found to be underage will have their accounts permanently removed immediately upon discovery.

2. Prohibited Content and Behaviour

The following are strictly prohibited on Meopel:

  • Any content that sexually exploits or abuses minors
  • Grooming, solicitation, or any attempt to exploit minors
  • Sharing, distributing, or requesting child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form
  • Any communication or activity that endangers the safety of a minor

3. Reporting

Any user who encounters content or behaviour that violates this policy must report it immediately using the in-app reporting tool or by emailing meopel.app@gmail.com. All reports are reviewed promptly by our team.

4. Enforcement

Violations of this policy will result in immediate and permanent account termination. Where required by law, we will report violations to the appropriate authorities, including the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) or relevant Indian authorities.

5. Platform Safeguards

  • All profile photos are scanned using face detection to verify a real person is present
  • Phone number verification is required for all accounts
  • Users can block and report anyone at any time
  • A reputation system silently flags and restricts suspicious behaviour

6. Contact

To report a child safety concern, email us immediately at meopel.app@gmail.com.
