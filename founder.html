The Founder

Building Meopel from Bangalore, India

Soni Sahu

Founder & CTO, Meopel

I've spent 10 years building products people actually use — as co-founder and CTO of Tweencraft, an animation app that reached 10 million users worldwide, featured in The Hindu, with no external funding.

I built Meopel because I kept running into the same problem: I wanted someone to grab coffee with, catch a match with, or just walk around the city with — and there was no good way to find that. Not dating. Just company. I looked for an app and couldn't find one, so I built it.

I like to ideate, work through problems with a small team, and ship things that work. Meopel is what that looks like when I do it for myself.
